About Me

Hello, I'm Allan Watatani. I am an Offensive Cyber Engineer and Software Engineer with a background in Computer Engineering. I have a strong interest in seeing how things work under the hood. These days, a lot of my work is focused on reverse engineering and vulnerability research of embedded Linux (and other) systems.

I studied computer engineering at Illinois Tech, where I earned my master's and bachelor's degrees. My interests have bounced around quite a bit over the last few years, from hardware design to OS design, machine learning, software development, and eventually offensive security.

Outside of engineering, basketball has been a big part of my life. I played five years of NCAA DIII basketball at Illinois Tech, and I still enjoy pickup at my local Lifetime with my brothers. I also like to play the piano. Right now, I am working on Turkish March by Mozart and Solfeggietto by C.P.E Bach. Solfeggietto is the piece that Skinny Pete played at the music shop in Breaking Bad.
